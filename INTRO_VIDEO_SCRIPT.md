## 📋 Key Concepts Summary

For your video, emphasize these **unique differentiators**:

### 1. Signal Desk Metaphor
- You're a **broadcaster**, not just a user
- Content is "transmitted", not "posted"
- Creates a sense of importance and intentionality

### 2. Signal Tiers (⚡ Flash / 📡 Pulse / 📻 Broadcast)
- **User controls content lifespan** — not the platform
- Different tiers for different purposes
- Visual distinction with icons and colors

### 3. Signal Heatmap
- **Real-time trending visualization**
- Heat percentages show actual engagement
- Color-coded for quick scanning

### 4. Signal Capsules
- Evolution of stories with **more control**
- Progress bars, tier indicators
- Live broadcast badges

### 5. Frequency Bands (Signals Page)
- Radio tuner UI for browsing signals
- Tune into: Breaking ⚡ | Trending 🔥 | Chill 🎵 | Broadcast 📡
- Smart auto-classification
